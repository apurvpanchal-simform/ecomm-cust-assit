## 1. Understanding Warranties

---

### Q1.1: What is a manufacturer's warranty?

**Answer:**
A **manufacturer's warranty** is a guarantee provided by the product's maker (not us) that the product will function as described for a specified period under normal use. If the product fails due to a **manufacturing defect** or **material fault** within the warranty period, the manufacturer will repair or replace it at no cost to you.

Key points to understand:
- The warranty is a contract between you and the **manufacturer**, not the retailer/ecommerce platform.
- Warranty terms, duration, and coverage vary significantly by brand and product category.
- The warranty generally covers **defects in materials and workmanship** — not damage caused by misuse, accidents, or unauthorized modifications.
- Warranty service is typically provided through the manufacturer's **authorized service centers**.

We display the warranty duration prominently on each product page. The warranty document/card is included in the product box.

---

### Q1.2: What's the difference between a manufacturer warranty and an extended warranty?

**Answer:**

| Feature | Manufacturer Warranty | Extended Warranty |
|---|---|---|
| Who provides it | Product manufacturer | Third-party insurer or our platform |
| Duration | Typically 1–2 years | 1–3 additional years |
| Cost | Included in product price | Paid separately |
| Coverage | Manufacturing defects | Defects + sometimes accidental damage |
| Starts | On purchase date | After manufacturer warranty ends |
| Service provider | Brand's authorized centers | Depends on plan |

The **manufacturer warranty** is free and covers factory defects. An **extended warranty** (purchased separately) kicks in after the manufacturer warranty expires and may offer broader or more convenient coverage.

## 2. Warranty Coverage

---

### Q2.1: What does a warranty typically cover?

**Answer:**
Most manufacturer warranties cover:

- **Manufacturing defects** — flaws in materials or assembly that cause the product to fail under normal use
- **Component failure** — hardware or mechanical failure not caused by external factors
- **Performance degradation** — product failing to meet stated specifications (e.g., a battery holding significantly less charge than rated)

**Examples of covered issues:**
- A laptop motherboard failure within 1 year of purchase
- A refrigerator compressor failing within its 5-year compressor warranty
- A TV screen developing dead pixels without physical damage
- A washing machine drum bearing wearing out under normal use

Always refer to the specific warranty card or the manufacturer's website for exact coverage terms for your product.

---

### Q2.2: What is NOT covered by a manufacturer's warranty?

**Answer:**
The following are typically **excluded** from manufacturer warranties:

- **Physical damage** — cracks, dents, broken screens from drops or impacts
- **Water damage** — unless the product is rated waterproof and the damage was within rated limits
- **Electrical damage** — from power surges, incorrect voltage, or improper use
- **Damage from unauthorized repairs** — repairs done outside authorized service centers
- **Cosmetic damage** — scratches, dents that don't affect functionality
- **Consumables** — batteries (after capacity degrades normally), printer ink/toner, filters
- **Normal wear and tear** — gradual degradation from regular use
- **Pest damage** — rodent chewing, insect damage
- **Loss or theft** — warranties don't cover missing products
- **Improper installation** — damage caused by incorrect setup
- **Software issues** — viruses, malware, software conflicts (in most cases)

If your product's issue falls into one of these categories, it may still be serviceable, but at your cost. Consider our **extended warranty with accidental damage cover** for broader protection.

---

### Q2.3: Does the warranty cover accidental damage?

**Answer:**
**Standard manufacturer warranties do NOT cover accidental damage** (drops, spills, cracks, etc.).

To get coverage for accidental damage, you need:

1. **Our Extended Warranty with Accidental Damage Protection (ADP)** — available for purchase at the time of buying the product or within the first 30 days.
2. **Manufacturer's own protection plans** — some brands (Apple, Samsung, etc.) offer their own paid protection plans (e.g., AppleCare+) that cover accidental damage.
3. **Home insurance or gadget insurance** — third-party insurance policies may cover gadgets against accidental damage, theft, and more.

Accidental Damage Protection plans are available at checkout for eligible categories. Check the product page for ADP availability and pricing.

## 3. Warranty Registration

---

### Q3.1: Do I need to register my product to activate the warranty?

**Answer:**
This depends on the brand. Some manufacturers require **product registration** to activate the full warranty, while others automatically cover any purchase with a valid purchase receipt.

**How to check:**
- The warranty card inside the product box will state if registration is required and how to do it.
- Most brands allow registration on their official website with the product's serial number and purchase date.

**Registering with us:**
For many brands, your purchase with us is automatically registered if you have an account. Go to **My Account → My Products** to see your registered products. If a brand requires self-registration, we'll send you a reminder email with the registration link within 24 hours of delivery.

**Even if registration isn't mandatory**, we recommend registering your product because:
- It helps the manufacturer locate service history faster.
- Some brands extend the warranty duration for registered users.
- It simplifies the warranty claim process.

---

### Q3.2: What details do I need to register my warranty?

**Answer:**
Typically required for warranty registration:

- **Product serial number** (found on the box or device itself — usually a sticker on the back/bottom)
- **Model number** (also on the box or product label)
- **Purchase date** (date of delivery)
- **Proof of purchase** (your invoice from us — downloadable from My Account → Orders)
- **Your contact details** (name, email, phone, address)

Keep your **original invoice** safe — it serves as proof of purchase for all warranty claims, regardless of whether you formally registered.

---

### Q3.3: I lost my warranty card. Can I still claim warranty?

**Answer:**
Yes. The warranty card is the physical document, but the **proof of purchase (invoice)** is what actually validates your warranty claim. Most manufacturers and service centers accept:

- **Original invoice** from the retailer (us) — downloadable from your account
- **Digital purchase confirmation** email

If a service center specifically asks for the warranty card and won't accept your invoice, contact our support team. We can provide a **warranty registration certificate** or official purchase letter confirming the purchase date, which most manufacturers accept.

---

**Answer:**
You can initiate a warranty claim in two ways:

**Via our platform (recommended):**
1. Log in to your account and go to **My Account → My Orders → [Order] → Claim Warranty**.
2. Describe the issue in detail.

4. Select your preferred resolution: **repair, replacement, or service center visit**.

**Directly with the manufacturer:**
1. Visit the manufacturer's official website and find their "Service/Support/Warranty" page.
2. Or call the manufacturer's customer care number (listed in your warranty card or on the product box).
3. Provide your product serial number, model, purchase date, and describe the issue.
4. They'll guide you to the nearest authorized service center or arrange a pickup.

Both routes are valid. Going through our platform gives you a smoother experience and our team advocates for you if there are any disputes.

Having this information ready will significantly speed up the claim process.

### Q4.3: What are my options when a warranty claim is approved?

**Answer:**
When a warranty claim is approved, the manufacturer or service center will offer one or more of these resolutions:

1. **Repair:** The defective component is fixed. You typically get the repaired device back within **7–21 business days** depending on part availability.

2. **Replacement (same product):** If the product is beyond repair or repair is not economical, the manufacturer replaces it with the same model (or equivalent if the model is discontinued).

3. **Exchange/Upgrade:** Some brands offer to exchange to a newer model if the exact model is unavailable, sometimes with a value adjustment.

4. **Refund (rare):** In some cases, especially early in the warranty period, a full or partial refund may be offered. This is at the manufacturer's discretion.

**Through our platform:** If the issue occurs within **30 days of delivery**, we may offer direct replacement or refund without going through the manufacturer. Contact support first.

### Q4.4: How long does it take to resolve a warranty claim?

**Answer:**
Timelines vary depending on the issue and resolution type:

- **Initial claim review:** 1–2 business days (by our team or manufacturer's support)

- **Diagnosis:** 3–7 business days at the service center
- **Repair:** 7–14 business days (longer if parts need to be ordered)

For urgent cases (essential devices like phones/laptops), many service centers offer **priority service** at an additional charge, or you can request a **loan device** (available with some brands and our extended warranty plans).

## 5. Warranty Voidance

---

### Q5.1: What actions will void my product warranty?

**Answer:**
The following typically void a manufacturer's warranty. Avoid these to keep your warranty valid:

- **Unauthorized repairs or modifications** — opening the device yourself or using a non-authorized repair shop
- **Removing or altering the serial number** — this is often illegal and always voids the warranty
- **Tampering with seals or stickers** — many devices have tamper-evident stickers inside or on screws
- **Rooting or jailbreaking** (for phones/tablets) — software modification may void warranty on some brands
- **Using third-party accessories that cause damage** — e.g., a non-certified charger that damages the battery
- **Physical damage** — the manufacturer may void the entire warranty if the device shows physical damage, arguing the damage may have caused the defect
- **Improper installation or use** — using the product in conditions or for purposes outside its specifications (e.g., using a non-outdoor appliance outdoors)
- **Damage caused by pests, floods, fire, or acts of nature** — unless covered by a specific protection plan

**In India**, the Consumer Protection Act provides some safeguards against manufacturers unfairly voiding warranties. If you believe your warranty was wrongly voided, you can raise a complaint with the manufacturer or approach a consumer forum.

---

### Q5.2: Does opening the product box void the warranty?

**Answer:**
No. Opening the product box and normal use of the product does not void the warranty. You have every right to use the product as intended. The warranty is voided only by the actions listed in Q5.1 (tampering, unauthorized repair, misuse, etc.) — not by simply using the product.

However, if you opened the product, used it, and then found a defect, the service center may inspect to confirm the defect was pre-existing (manufacturing fault) vs. caused by use.

---

### Q5.3: I got my product repaired at a local shop and now the manufacturer won't honor the warranty. What can I do?

**Answer:**
Unauthorized repair typically voids the warranty. If this has happened:

1. **Contact the manufacturer** and request they reconsider if the issue is clearly unrelated to the repair performed.
2. **Contact us** — if the product was still within its first 30 days when the defect first appeared, we may be able to assist through our platform guarantee.
3. **Consumer Protection Act (India)** — If you believe the manufacturer is being unreasonable, you can file a complaint at the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

Going forward: Always use **authorized service centers** for repairs. Most manufacturers list authorized centers on their official website by city/pin code.

## 8. Warranty by Product Category

---

### Q8.1: What warranty applies to smartphones and tablets?

**Answer:**
- **Standard:** 1-year manufacturer warranty against manufacturing defects.
- **Battery:** Usually 6 months (batteries degrade naturally and are often excluded from full 1-year coverage after 6 months).
- **Screen:** Covered under manufacturing defects but NOT for physical cracks or drops.
- **Water resistance:** If the device is IP-rated, warranty covers water ingress up to the rated specification.

**Claim route:** Visit the brand's authorized service center (Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, etc. all have dedicated centers in major cities). Through our platform, you can initiate the claim and we'll coordinate.

---

### Q8.2: What warranty applies to home appliances (refrigerators, ACs, washing machines)?

**Answer:**
Home appliances typically have layered warranties:

- **Product warranty:** 1–2 years comprehensive (parts + labour + in-home service)
- **Motor/compressor warranty:** 5–10 years (specific to the compressor/motor component only)
- **Parts warranty:** 1–5 years

**In-home service:** For large appliances, service is done at your home — you don't take it to a service center. A technician visits your address.

**Installation warranty:** If we or the manufacturer arranged installation, the installation work is also covered under warranty for 90 days.

---

### Q8.3: What warranty applies to furniture?

**Answer:**
Furniture warranties vary by type and brand:

- **Structural warranty:** 1–5 years against defects in frame integrity, joint failures, and material delamination.
- **Fabric/upholstery:** Often 1 year against material defects (not stains or normal wear).
- **Mechanism warranty (recliners, sofa-beds):** 1–2 years.

**What's excluded from furniture warranty:**
- Damage from moisture or improper care
- Color fading from sunlight (unless outdoor-rated)
- Normal wear on high-contact areas
- Scratches and surface marks from use

For furniture warranty claims, a technician inspection at your home is typically arranged before replacement or repair is authorized.

---

### Q8.4: Do clothing and footwear come with a warranty?

**Answer:**
Clothing and footwear generally do not carry formal warranties in the traditional sense. However:

- Our standard **30-day return policy** covers manufacturing defects (stitching failures, material defects, color bleeding on first wash, sole detachment, etc.).
- Premium or luxury brands may offer their own quality guarantees.
- Some performance sportswear brands (e.g., specific outdoor gear) may carry limited warranties.

If you receive clothing or footwear with an immediate manufacturing defect, report it within 30 days under our return policy.

## 9. Consumer Rights & Escalation

---

### Q9.1: What can I do if a manufacturer refuses to honor a valid warranty claim?

**Answer:**
If a manufacturer is refusing a legitimate warranty claim, you have several options:

1. **Escalate within the manufacturer** — Ask to speak to a senior customer service representative or the brand's grievance officer.
2. **Contact us** — We can intervene as the retailer. We have commercial relationships with brands and can often resolve disputes that individual customers cannot.
3. **Consumer Forum (India)** — File a complaint with the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (District, State, or National depending on product value) under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**.
4. **National Consumer Helpline** — Call 1800-11-4000 or 14404 (toll-free) to report a consumer dispute.
5. **Brand escalation portal** — Many brands have online grievance portals mandated by Indian law.

We are committed to helping you get what you're legally entitled to. Contact us at **warranty-support@ourstore.com** and describe the dispute — our team will do everything we can to advocate for you.

---

### Q9.2: What are my rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (India) regarding defective products?

**Answer:**
Under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**, you are entitled to:

- **Replacement** of the defective product with a new product of the same description.
- **Refund** of the price paid for the product.
- **Compensation** for any loss or injury suffered due to the defect.
- **Repair** of defects in the product at no charge.

These rights apply **regardless of any warranty limitations** the manufacturer may claim. If a product has a defect that was present at the time of purchase, you can claim these remedies.

**Time limits:** Consumer complaints can typically be filed within **2 years** of the cause of action arising. Don't delay escalation.
