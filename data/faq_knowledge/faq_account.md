## 1. Account Creation & Registration

---

### Q1.1: How do I create an account?

**Answer:**
Creating an account is free and takes less than two minutes. Follow these steps:

1. Click the **"Sign Up"** or **"Create Account"** button on the top-right corner of the homepage.
2. Enter your **full name**, **email address**, and a **secure password**.
3. Optionally, you can sign up using your **Google**, **Facebook**, or **Apple** account via the social login buttons.
4. Check your inbox for a **verification email** and click the confirmation link.
5. Once verified, you'll be redirected to your new account dashboard.

**Notes:**
- Your email address must be unique — you cannot register two accounts with the same email.
- Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
- If you don't receive the verification email within 5 minutes, check your spam/junk folder.

---

### Q1.2: Can I create an account without an email address?

**Answer:**
Currently, an email address is required to register an account. This is used for order confirmations, shipping notifications, security alerts, and password recovery. We do not support phone-number-only registration at this time.

If you prefer not to use your primary email, you may use a secondary or alias email address — we only use it for transactional and account-related communications, not marketing (unless you opt in).

---

### Q1.3: I didn't receive my verification email. What should I do?

**Answer:**
If your verification email hasn't arrived:

1. **Check your spam, junk, or promotions folder** — automated emails sometimes get filtered.
2. Wait up to **10 minutes** — delivery delays can occur during high-traffic periods.
3. **Resend the verification email**: Go to the login page, enter your email, and click "Resend verification email."
4. Ensure you entered the **correct email address** during registration. If you made a typo, you'll need to re-register with the correct email.
5. If none of the above works, contact our support team at **support@ourstore.com** with your registered email, and we'll manually verify your account.

---

### Q1.4: Can I have multiple accounts?

**Answer:**
Each customer is permitted **one account per email address**. However, you may create separate accounts using different email addresses (e.g., personal vs. business). Please note:

- Attempting to abuse multi-account policies (e.g., to claim multiple new-customer discounts) may result in all associated accounts being suspended.
- If you're managing purchases for a business, consider our **Business Account** program, which supports multiple users under a single organization.

---

### Q1.5: Is there a minimum age requirement to register?

**Answer:**
Yes. You must be at least **18 years old** to create an account and make purchases on our platform. By registering, you confirm that you meet this requirement. If we have reason to believe an account was created by a minor, it may be suspended pending verification.

## 2. Login & Authentication

---

### Q2.1: How do I log into my account?

**Answer:**
To log in:

1. Click **"Sign In"** on the top-right of any page.
2. Enter your **registered email address** and **password**.
3. Click **"Log In"**.

You may also use **Social Login** (Google, Facebook, Apple) if that's how you originally registered.

**Tip:** Check the "Remember me" box on trusted devices to stay logged in across sessions.

---

### Q2.2: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

**Answer:**
To reset your password:

1. Go to the **Login page** and click **"Forgot Password?"**
2. Enter your registered email address and click **"Send Reset Link"**.
3. Check your inbox for a **password reset email** (check spam if needed).
4. Click the link in the email — it is valid for **60 minutes**.
5. Enter and confirm your new password.
6. Log in with your new credentials.

**If you no longer have access to your registered email**, contact support with proof of identity (order history, billing address, phone number) and we'll help you regain access.

---

### Q2.3: My account is locked. What happened?

**Answer:**
Accounts are temporarily locked after **5 consecutive failed login attempts** to protect against unauthorized access. The lockout lasts **30 minutes**.

To unlock immediately:
- Use the **"Forgot Password"** flow to reset your password.
- Contact support if you believe the lockout was triggered by someone else trying to access your account.

---

### Q2.4: Can I stay logged in on my device?

**Answer:**
Yes. When logging in, check the **"Remember Me"** box and your session will persist for up to **30 days** on that device (unless you log out manually).

For security reasons, we recommend not enabling this on:
- Shared or public computers
- Devices that others have access to

Your session may also be terminated automatically if suspicious activity is detected.

---

### Q2.5: Why was I automatically logged out?

**Answer:**
You may be logged out automatically for the following reasons:

- **Session expiry** — sessions on inactive tabs expire after 2 hours of inactivity.
- **Password change** — changing your password logs out all other active sessions.
- **Security alert** — unusual login activity may trigger an automatic logout.
- **Account suspension** — if your account has been flagged or suspended.
- **Browser data cleared** — clearing cookies/cache will log you out.

## 3. Password & Security

---

### Q3.1: How do I change my password?

**Answer:**
To change your password when you're already logged in:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Security → Change Password**.
2. Enter your **current password**.
3. Enter your **new password** and confirm it.
4. Click **"Save Changes"**.

You'll receive a confirmation email to your registered address. All other active sessions will be logged out automatically.

---

### Q3.2: What are the password requirements?

**Answer:**
Your password must:
- Be at least **8 characters** long
- Contain at least **one uppercase letter** (A–Z)
- Contain at least **one lowercase letter** (a–z)
- Contain at least **one number** (0–9)
- Contain at least **one special character** (e.g., `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`, `%`)
- Not be the same as your last 3 passwords

**Recommended:** Use a passphrase or a password manager to create and store strong, unique passwords.

---

### Q3.3: What is two-factor authentication (2FA) and how do I enable it?

**Answer:**
Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security by requiring a second verification step beyond your password. Even if someone knows your password, they cannot access your account without the second factor.

**To enable 2FA:**
1. Go to **Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**.
2. Choose your preferred method:
   - **Authenticator App** (e.g., Google Authenticator, Authy) — recommended
   - **SMS code** to your phone number
3. Follow the on-screen setup instructions.
4. Save your **backup codes** in a safe place — these are used if you lose access to your 2FA device.

**To disable 2FA:** Return to the same settings page and click "Disable 2FA." You'll need to verify your identity first.

---

### Q3.4: I lost access to my two-factor authentication device. What do I do?

**Answer:**
If you can't access your 2FA device:

1. On the login page, click **"Use a backup code"** and enter one of the backup codes you saved during 2FA setup.
2. If you don't have backup codes, contact our support team. We'll verify your identity through:
   - Recent order details
   - Billing address
   - Photo ID (for high-security recovery)
3. Once verified, we'll help you disable 2FA and regain access.

**Note:** This process can take 24–48 hours for security reasons.

---

### Q3.5: How do I recognize a phishing or fraudulent email claiming to be from your store?

**Answer:**
Phishing emails mimic legitimate communications to steal your credentials. Our official emails will:
- Always come from **@ourstore.com** domains only
- Never ask for your password via email
- Never include suspicious attachments

**Red flags to watch for:**
- Email from an unrecognized or misspelled domain
- Urgent language like "your account will be deleted in 24 hours"
- Links that don't lead to **www.ourstore.com**
- Requests for your password, credit card, or personal ID via email

If you receive a suspicious email, **do not click any links**. Forward it to **security@ourstore.com** so we can investigate.

## 4. Profile Management

---

### Q4.1: How do I update my personal information?

**Answer:**
To update your name, phone number, or date of birth:

1. Log in and go to **Account Settings → Personal Information**.
2. Edit the desired fields.
3. Click **"Save Changes"**.

**Note:** Changing your name may require identity verification for accounts with a history of completed purchases, to prevent fraud.

---

### Q4.2: How do I change my email address?

**Answer:**
To change your registered email address:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Personal Information → Email Address**.
2. Click **"Change Email"**.
3. Enter your **new email address** and your **current password** to confirm.
4. A verification link will be sent to the **new email address**.
5. Click the link to confirm the change — until you do, your old email remains active.

**Important:** You cannot use an email address that is already linked to another account.

---

### Q4.3: Can I add multiple addresses to my account?

**Answer:**
Yes. You can save multiple shipping and billing addresses in your account:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Addresses → Add New Address**.
2. Fill in the address details and optionally set it as your **default shipping** or **default billing** address.
3. Click **"Save Address"**.

You can save up to **10 addresses** on your account. During checkout, you can choose any saved address or enter a new one.

---

### Q4.4: How do I upload or change my profile picture?

**Answer:**
1. Go to **Account Settings → Profile**.
2. Click on your current avatar or the **"Upload Photo"** button.
3. Select an image file from your device (JPG, PNG, or GIF — max 5 MB).
4. Crop or adjust as needed and confirm.

Your profile photo is visible only to you in your account dashboard — it is not publicly displayed.

---

### Q4.5: Can I change my username?

**Answer:**
If your account uses a public username (relevant for review features or community sections), you can change it once every **90 days**:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Profile → Username**.
2. Enter your desired new username (3–20 characters, letters and numbers only).
3. Click **"Check Availability"** and then **"Save"**.

Your email address, which is used to log in, is separate from your display username.

## 5. Communication Preferences & Notifications

---

### Q5.1: How do I manage my email notifications and marketing preferences?

**Answer:**
To manage what emails you receive:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Notifications & Preferences**.
2. Toggle on or off the categories you want:
   - **Order updates** (shipping, delivery, returns) — always enabled
   - **Promotional offers and sales** — opt in/out
   - **New arrivals and recommendations** — opt in/out
   - **Newsletter** — opt in/out
   - **Account security alerts** — always enabled

Alternatively, every marketing email contains an **"Unsubscribe"** link at the bottom. Clicking it removes you from all marketing communications within **10 business days**.

**Note:** Transactional emails (order confirmations, password resets) cannot be disabled as they are essential to your account.

---

### Q5.2: How do I enable or disable push notifications?

**Answer:**
**On mobile app:**
1. Go to **App Settings → Notifications**.
2. Toggle the notification categories you want to receive.

**Via browser:**
When you first visit our site, a prompt may appear asking for notification permissions. You can manage this in your **browser settings** (usually under Site Settings → Notifications → ourstore.com).

---

### Q5.3: I keep receiving emails after unsubscribing. What should I do?

**Answer:**
Unsubscribing can take up to **10 business days** to fully process due to email queue systems. If you're still receiving marketing emails after that period:

1. Check that you unsubscribed from the correct email address (you may have multiple accounts).
2. Contact us at **support@ourstore.com** with the subject line "Unsubscribe Request" and we'll manually remove you from all lists immediately.

Transactional emails (like order confirmations) cannot be stopped as they're required for your purchases.

## 6. Linked Accounts & Social Login

---

### Q6.1: How do I link or unlink a social account (Google, Facebook, Apple)?

**Answer:**
**To link a social account:**
1. Go to **Account Settings → Linked Accounts**.
2. Click **"Connect"** next to Google, Facebook, or Apple.
3. Authorize the connection in the popup window.

**To unlink:**
1. Go to the same page and click **"Disconnect"** next to the linked account.
2. Ensure you have a password set before unlinking, otherwise you'll be locked out.

---

### Q6.2: I signed up with Google but now want to use an email/password login. How?

**Answer:**
1. Go to **Account Settings → Security → Set Password**.
2. Enter and confirm a new password.
3. Once a password is set, you can log in using either your email/password or Google.
4. Optionally, you can then unlink Google from **Linked Accounts** if you prefer.

## 7. Account Deactivation & Deletion

---

### Q7.1: How do I temporarily deactivate my account?

**Answer:**
To temporarily deactivate your account:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Privacy → Deactivate Account**.
2. Select a reason from the dropdown.
3. Confirm with your password.

While deactivated:
- Your profile and order history are hidden
- You will not receive marketing emails
- You can reactivate at any time by logging in again

**Note:** Deactivation does not cancel any active subscriptions — do that separately before deactivating.

---

### Q7.2: How do I permanently delete my account?

**Answer:**
To permanently delete your account:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Privacy → Delete Account**.
2. Read the information about what deletion means.
3. Confirm with your password and click **"Delete My Account"**.

**What gets deleted:**
- Personal profile data (name, address, phone number)
- Saved payment methods
- Wishlist and saved items

**What is retained (for legal/financial compliance):**
- Order history and transaction records (retained for 7 years per tax law)
- Reviews you've submitted (anonymized)

Account deletion is **permanent and irreversible**. You will need to create a new account if you wish to shop with us in the future.

---

### Q7.3: Can I recover a deleted account?

**Answer:**
Deleted accounts **cannot be recovered**. All personal data is permanently removed within **30 days** of the deletion request (except records retained for legal compliance). If you deleted your account by mistake and it's been less than 24 hours, contact support immediately at **support@ourstore.com** — in some cases we may be able to halt the deletion process.

## 8. Account Security & Suspicious Activity

---

### Q8.1: I think someone else is accessing my account. What should I do?

**Answer:**
Take these steps immediately:

1. **Change your password** via Account Settings → Security.
2. **Enable 2FA** if not already active.
3. **Review active sessions** under Account Settings → Security → Active Sessions, and click **"Log Out All Other Devices"**.
4. **Review recent orders** for any unauthorized purchases.
5. **Contact support** at security@ourstore.com to flag the account for review.

If unauthorized purchases were made, we'll work with you to investigate and, where applicable, issue a refund.

---

### Q8.2: Where can I see all active sessions on my account?

**Answer:**
1. Go to **Account Settings → Security → Active Sessions**.
2. You'll see a list of devices/browsers that are currently logged in, including:
   - Device type and operating system
   - Approximate location (city/country)
   - Last active time
3. Click **"Revoke"** next to any session you don't recognize, or **"Log Out All Other Devices"** to clear all sessions except your current one.

## 9. Loyalty, Rewards & Referrals

---

### Q9.1: How does the loyalty/rewards program work?

**Answer:**
Our loyalty program rewards you for every purchase:

- Earn **1 point for every ₹100 spent** (or currency equivalent).
- Points are credited to your account within **24 hours** of order delivery.
- Redeem points during checkout — **100 points = ₹10 discount**.
- Points expire after **12 months** of account inactivity.

To view your points balance: Go to **Account → Rewards Dashboard**.

---

### Q9.2: How do I refer a friend and earn rewards?

**Answer:**
1. Go to **Account → Refer a Friend**.
2. Copy your unique referral link or share it via email/WhatsApp/social media.
3. When your friend registers using your link and completes their **first purchase**, you both receive a reward:
   - **You:** ₹200 store credit
   - **Your friend:** ₹100 off their first order

Referral rewards are credited within **7 days** of your friend's qualifying purchase. There is no limit on the number of referrals you can make.

## 10. Business & Corporate Accounts

---

### Q10.1: Do you offer business or bulk-buying accounts?

**Answer:**
Yes. Our **Business Account** program is designed for corporate buyers, resellers, and bulk purchasers. Benefits include:

- **GST invoice generation** for every order
- **Bulk pricing discounts** on select product categories
- **Dedicated account manager**
- **Net-30 payment terms** (subject to credit approval)
- **Multi-user access** under a single business account

To apply, visit **ourstore.com/business** or email **business@ourstore.com** with your company details and estimated monthly spend.

---

### Q10.2: How do I get GST invoices for my purchases?

**Answer:**
To receive GST-compliant invoices:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Billing Information**.
2. Enter your **GSTIN (GST Identification Number)** and business name.
3. Save changes.

All future orders will automatically include a GST invoice. For past orders, go to **Order History**, select the order, and click **"Download GST Invoice"**. If the option isn't available, contact support with your order number and GSTIN.
