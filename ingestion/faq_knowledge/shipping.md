# Shipping Policy

## Overview

We deliver products across supported domestic and international locations through trusted logistics partners.

---

## Shipping Methods

### Standard Shipping

* Delivery Time: 3–7 Business Days
* Economical option

### Express Shipping

* Delivery Time: 1–3 Business Days
* Additional charges apply

### Same-Day Delivery

Available only in selected cities and for eligible products.

---

## Order Processing

Orders are processed:

* Monday to Friday
* 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Processing typically takes:

* 24–48 hours

Orders placed on weekends or holidays may experience delays.

---

## Shipping Charges

Shipping costs depend on:

* Delivery location
* Product weight
* Package dimensions
* Shipping method selected

Free shipping may be offered for eligible orders.

---

## Order Tracking

Customers receive:

* Order confirmation email
* Shipment confirmation email
* Tracking number

Tracking status examples:

* Processing
* Packed
* Shipped
* In Transit
* Out for Delivery
* Delivered
* Delayed

---

## Delivery Delays

Possible causes:

* Weather conditions
* Natural disasters
* Customs clearance
* High seasonal demand
* Carrier disruptions

---

## Failed Delivery Attempts

Carriers may attempt delivery multiple times.

If delivery repeatedly fails:

* Package may be returned to warehouse
* Customer support will contact customer

---

## International Shipping

International shipments may incur:

* Customs duties
* Import taxes
* Regulatory fees

These charges are typically the customer's responsibility.

---

## Lost Shipments

A shipment may be considered lost if:

* No tracking updates for extended period
* Carrier confirms loss

Investigation timeline:

* 5–10 business days

Possible outcomes:

* Replacement
* Refund
* Store credit

---

## Address Changes

Address changes are allowed only before shipment dispatch.

Once shipped, modifications may not be possible.
