# 🛍️ E-Commerce Assistant

Welcome to your **Intelligent E-Commerce Customer Support Assistant**! 

This AI-powered assistant is designed to help you with all your shopping needs, combining real-time database access with advanced visual search capabilities.

## 🌟 What can I do?

- **📦 Track Orders:** Ask about your recent purchases or check shipping statuses (e.g., *"Where is my order?"* or use the `/orders` command).
- **🛍️ Browse Products:** Ask for recommendations or view our catalog (e.g., *"Show me some electronics"* or use the `/products` command).
- **📸 Visual Search:** Upload an image of a product you like, and I'll find similar items in our store!
- **❓ FAQs & Support:** Ask questions about our return policies, shipping times, or general support.

---

*Tip: You can use the `/orders` and `/products` commands at any time for instant access to your data!*
