## 1. Shipping Options & Timelines

---

### Q1.1: What shipping options are available?

**Answer:**
We offer the following shipping methods, available at checkout based on your location and cart contents:

**Standard Shipping:**
- Estimated delivery: **4–7 business days**
- Cost: ₹49–₹99 depending on order weight and location
- Free on orders above **₹499**

**Express Shipping:**
- Estimated delivery: **1–3 business days**
- Cost: ₹99–₹199 depending on order weight and location
- Available in metros and Tier-1 cities

**Same-Day Delivery:**
- Available in select cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai
- Order must be placed before **12:00 PM** for same-day delivery
- Cost: ₹149 flat
- Not available for all product categories

**Scheduled Delivery:**
- Choose a preferred delivery date and time slot (morning/afternoon/evening)
- Available for large items (furniture, appliances) in select areas
- Cost: Included with product or ₹199 for general items

**Free Shipping:**
- Automatically applied on orders above **₹499** (standard delivery only)

At checkout, all available options for your pin code and cart will be shown with estimated dates.

---

### Q1.2: How are delivery timelines calculated?

**Answer:**
Delivery timelines are calculated from the **order dispatch date** (not the order placement date). Here's the breakdown:

1. **Order processing time:** 0–1 business day (most orders are processed within hours).
2. **Transit time:** Varies by distance and shipping method.
   - Metro to metro: 1–3 days
   - Metro to Tier-2 city: 3–5 days
   - Remote or rural areas: 5–10 days
3. **Holidays and weekends** are not counted as business days.
4. Orders placed after **5:00 PM** are typically dispatched the **next business day**.

The estimated delivery date shown at checkout accounts for processing + transit time. It is an estimate, not a guarantee — unforeseen logistics issues (weather, strikes, etc.) can occasionally cause delays.

### Q1.3: Do you ship to all pin codes in India?

**Answer:**
We ship to **over 27,000 pin codes** across India. However, service availability varies:

- **Metro and Tier-1 cities** — All shipping options available, fastest delivery times.
- **Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities** — Standard and sometimes express shipping available.
- **Remote/rural areas** — Standard shipping only, longer delivery times (7–14 days).
- **Some pin codes** — May be serviceable only for certain product categories or weights.

To check serviceability for your pin code, enter it on the product page or in your cart before checkout. If your pin code is not serviceable, we'll let you know and suggest the nearest serviceable location.

---

### Q1.4: Why is my delivery estimate longer than what was shown?

**Answer:**
Estimated delivery dates can change after order placement for these reasons:

- **Dispatch delay** — Item was out of stock or needed additional processing time.
- **Courier delay** — Logistics partner experienced high volumes, weather disruptions, or transit hub delays.
- **Wrong address** — Incomplete or incorrect address caused a re-routing attempt.
- **Holiday period** — Festive seasons or national holidays can add 1–3 days.
- **Payment verification** — For certain payment methods, orders may be held pending verification.

Check your order tracking page for real-time status. If delivery is more than 3 days beyond the promised date, contact support for resolution.

## 2. Shipping Costs

---

### Q2.1: How are shipping charges calculated?

**Answer:**
Shipping charges are calculated based on:

1. **Order value** — Free standard shipping on orders above ₹499.
2. **Delivery location** — Longer distances or remote areas may incur higher charges.
3. **Package weight and dimensions** — Volumetric weight may be used for bulky, lightweight items.
4. **Shipping method** — Standard, express, same-day each have different rates.
5. **Product type** — Some products (e.g., large furniture, heavy appliances) have fixed delivery charges.

The exact shipping fee is always displayed transparently at checkout before payment. No hidden charges are added after order placement.

## 3. Order Tracking

---

### Q3.1: How do I track my order?

**Answer:**
To track your order:

**Via your account:**
1. Log in and go to **My Account → My Orders**.
2. Click on the order you want to track.
3. Click **"Track Order"** — you'll see real-time status updates.

**Via email/SMS:**
- When your order is dispatched, you'll receive a shipping confirmation with a tracking link.
- Click the link to see current status on the courier's website.

**Via tracking number:**
- Your tracking number is available in your order details and dispatch email.
- Go directly to the courier partner's website (Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, Ekart, etc.) and enter the tracking number.

**Tracking statuses explained:**
- **Order Confirmed** — Payment received, order in queue.
- **Processing** — Item being packed and prepared for dispatch.
- **Dispatched** — Order handed to logistics partner.
- **In Transit** — Package en route to destination.
- **Out for Delivery** — Package with delivery agent, arriving today.
- **Delivered** — Package delivered to the address.
- **Delivery Attempted** — Delivery tried but unsuccessful (see Q5.1).

---

### Q3.2: The tracking page shows "in transit" for several days. Is my package stuck?

**Answer:**
A prolonged "in transit" status can mean:

- **Package is moving between transit hubs** — not every movement updates the tracker in real time.
- **Custom hold** — Rare for domestic shipments, but can happen for certain goods.
- **Logistics partner delay** — High-volume periods, weather, or hub congestion.
- **Tracking update lag** — The courier's system may be slow to update.

**What to do:**
- Wait **2–3 additional business days** past the expected delivery date before escalating.
- If the status hasn't changed for **5+ days**, contact us with your order number. We'll raise an investigation with the courier.
- If the package is confirmed lost in transit, we'll dispatch a **replacement or full refund** within 5 business days.

---

### Q3.3: My tracking shows "Delivered" but I haven't received my package. What should I do?

**Answer:**
This situation (also called a "false delivery") can happen. Here's what to do:

1. **Check your surroundings** — Did a neighbor or family member accept it? Is it behind a door, in a mailbox, or with your building reception?
2. **Check within 24 hours** — Sometimes the delivery agent marks it delivered upon leaving it at the door/lobby.
3. **Contact the courier directly** — Use the tracking number to call the courier partner's helpline. Ask for the delivery agent's details.
4. If still unresolved after 24–48 hours, **contact our support** with your order number. We'll:
   - Review GPS/proof of delivery submitted by the courier.
   - Raise a formal dispute with the logistics partner.
   - Issue a replacement or refund if the package cannot be located within 5 business days.

## 4. Address Management & Changes

---

### Q4.1: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

**Answer:**
Address changes are possible only **before the order is dispatched**:

1. Go to **My Orders → [Order] → Edit Address**.
2. Enter or select the new delivery address.
3. Click **"Save"** — the change will be reflected in your order.

**After dispatch:** Address changes are typically not possible once the package is with the courier. Contact us immediately at support@ourstore.com with your order number — in some cases, we can request the courier to redirect the package, but this is not guaranteed and may add 1–3 days to delivery.

**If delivery fails due to incorrect address:** The package will be returned to our warehouse and you'll receive a refund, minus the shipping cost in some cases.

---

### Q4.2: Can I ship to a different address than my billing address?

**Answer:**
Yes. During checkout, you can specify a **separate billing address and shipping address**. This is common for gifts or business purchases. Simply:

1. At checkout, enter your shipping address.
2. Uncheck **"Same as billing address"** if shown.
3. Enter your billing address separately.

Both addresses are saved independently. Future orders will remember your saved addresses.

---

### Q4.3: Can I ship to a P.O. Box?

**Answer:**
Our logistics partners require a physical delivery address with a contact name and phone number. **P.O. Boxes are not accepted** as delivery addresses. If you use a P.O. Box, your order may be returned or delivery attempts may fail.

For people without a permanent address, we recommend:
- A workplace address
- A nearby friend's or family member's address
- A click-and-collect locker location (if available in your area — see Q4.4)

---

### Q4.4: Do you offer locker or pick-up point delivery?

**Answer:**
Yes, in select cities we offer delivery to **parcel locker locations** and **pickup points** (partner stores, kiosks):

- During checkout, select **"Pickup from Location"** and enter your pin code.
- Available pickup points near you will be shown on a map.
- Once your order arrives at the pickup point, you'll receive an SMS/email with a QR code or PIN.
- Collect your parcel within **3–5 days** (after which it may be returned).

This option is great if you're frequently unavailable at home during delivery hours.

## 5. Delivery Failures & Missed Deliveries

---

### Q5.1: The delivery was attempted but I wasn't home. What happens now?

**Answer:**
If a delivery attempt is missed:

1. The delivery agent will typically leave a **"Delivery Attempted" notification** via SMS or a physical slip.
2. **Re-delivery:** Most couriers will attempt delivery **up to 3 times** before returning the package.
3. You can reschedule delivery via:
   - The tracking link in your SMS/email
   - The courier's website using your tracking number
   - Contacting our customer support

4. **Pick-up from courier facility:** You may also be given the option to collect from the nearest courier hub. Bring a valid ID and your tracking number.

If all 3 delivery attempts fail, the package is returned to our warehouse. You'll then have the option to place a new order (re-delivery fee may apply) or receive a refund.

---

### Q5.2: What happens if my delivery address is unreachable?

**Answer:**
If the courier reports the address as unreachable (e.g., no access to gated community, remote area, unclear directions):

1. You'll receive an SMS/email notification with a contact number for the local delivery agent.
2. **Call the delivery agent directly** to coordinate access or alternative instructions.
3. Update your address notes in your account with gate codes, landmarks, or special instructions for future orders.

If the courier is unable to reach your location despite coordination attempts, the package will be returned and refunded.

---

### Q5.3: What should I do if my package was delivered to the wrong address?

**Answer:**
If your tracking shows delivered but to a wrong location:

1. Go to **My Orders → Report Issue → Wrong Delivery Location**.
2. Provide your order number and describe the situation.
3. We'll contact the logistics partner immediately.

In most cases, the delivery partner will retrieve the package and re-deliver it within **1–3 business days**. If retrieval is not possible, we'll dispatch a **replacement order** at no additional cost or issue a full refund.

### Q7.3: Will I have to pay customs duties or import taxes for international orders?

**Answer:**
Yes. International orders may be subject to **customs duties, import taxes, and VAT** levied by the destination country's customs authority. These are **not included** in the price you pay us.

**Who pays customs fees:** The recipient (you) is responsible for all import duties and taxes in the destination country.

**How much:** Varies widely by country. The USA typically doesn't charge duties on packages below $800; the EU charges VAT on imports. Check your country's customs website for specific thresholds.

**Delays:** Customs clearance can add **2–10 business days** to international delivery times.

**What we do:** We provide accurate item descriptions and declared values on all shipment paperwork. We do not undervalue parcels to avoid customs — this is illegal and puts you at risk.

### Q7.4: Can I track my international shipment?

**Answer:**
Yes. International orders are trackable from dispatch to delivery:

1. You'll receive a tracking number and link via email after dispatch.
2. Tracking covers Indian outbound leg, international hub transit, and local delivery in the destination country (where available).
3. Tracking updates may be slower in some countries — expect 24–48 hour gaps between updates.

For shipments via FedEx, DHL, or Aramex, you can track directly on their websites using the provided number.

## 8. Shipping for Special Orders

---

### Q8.1: How does shipping work for large/bulky items (furniture, appliances)?

**Answer:**
Large and heavy items are shipped via specialized logistics partners:

- **Scheduled delivery:** You'll be contacted to choose a delivery date and time window (typically 2-hour slots).
- **Installation service:** Optional paid installation available for appliances and furniture in select cities.
- **Delivery to doorstep:** Standard delivery brings items to your door or building lobby. Inside delivery (to a specific room) is a separate paid service.
- **Team delivery:** Items above 50 kg are delivered by a two-person team.

Timelines for large items are **7–14 business days** from order confirmation, longer in remote areas.

---

### Q8.2: How does shipping work for pre-order or back-order items?

**Answer:**
For **pre-order items** (not yet released) or **back-order items** (temporarily out of stock):

- Estimated dispatch date is shown on the product page and in your order confirmation.
- Your payment is authorized at order placement but **charged only when the item ships**.
- You'll receive a dispatch notification email when the item ships, followed by tracking details.
- You can **cancel a pre-order** anytime before dispatch from **My Orders → Cancel Item**.
- Delivery timelines after dispatch are the same as standard shipping.
