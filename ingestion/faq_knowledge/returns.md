# Return Policy

## Overview

We want customers to be completely satisfied with their purchases. If you are not happy with a product, you may request a return subject to the conditions outlined below.

---

## Return Eligibility

A product is eligible for return if:

* The return request is initiated within 30 calendar days of delivery.
* The item is unused and in its original condition.
* All original packaging, accessories, manuals, and tags are included.
* Proof of purchase is available.

---

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

* Gift cards
* Downloadable software products
* Personalized or customized products
* Perishable goods
* Products marked as Final Sale
* Hygiene-sensitive products after opening

---

## Damaged or Defective Products

If a product arrives damaged or defective:

1. Report the issue within 7 days of delivery.
2. Provide:

   * Order number
   * Product images
   * Description of the issue
3. Customer support will review the claim and provide resolution options.

Possible resolutions include:

* Replacement
* Repair
* Refund
* Store credit

---

## Return Process

### Step 1: Initiate Return

Contact customer support through:

* Support Portal
* Email
* Live Chat

Provide:

* Order ID
* Product name
* Reason for return

### Step 2: Return Authorization

Once approved, a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number will be generated.

### Step 3: Ship Product

Pack the item securely and include:

* RMA Number
* Original invoice
* Accessories

### Step 4: Inspection

Returned items undergo inspection before approval.

---

## Refund Policy

Refunds are issued after successful inspection.

Refund timelines:

| Payment Method | Processing Time    |
| -------------- | ------------------ |
| Credit Card    | 5–10 Business Days |
| Debit Card     | 5–10 Business Days |
| UPI            | 2–5 Business Days  |
| Net Banking    | 3–7 Business Days  |
| Store Credit   | Within 24 Hours    |

---

## Exchange Policy

Eligible products may be exchanged for:

* Different size
* Different color
* Replacement of defective product

Exchange availability depends on inventory.

---

## Return Shipping Charges

| Scenario                   | Shipping Cost |
| -------------------------- | ------------- |
| Defective Product          | Company Pays  |
| Wrong Product Delivered    | Company Pays  |
| Customer Preference Return | Customer Pays |

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I return an opened product?

Yes, if the product is defective and reported within the allowed period.

### Can I return after 30 days?

Generally no, unless covered under warranty or approved as an exception.

### When will I receive my refund?

After inspection and approval, refunds are processed according to the payment method used.
