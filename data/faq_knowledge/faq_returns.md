## 1. Return Policy Overview

---

### Q1.1: What is your return policy?

**Answer:**
We offer a **30-day return window** from the date of delivery for most items. To be eligible for a return:

- The item must be **unused, unworn, and in its original condition**.
- All **original packaging, tags, labels, and accessories** must be included.
- The item must not fall under our **non-returnable categories** (see Q1.3).
- A valid **order number or proof of purchase** must be provided.

**Return reasons we accept:**
- Defective or damaged item
- Wrong item received
- Item doesn't match description/images on the website
- Size or fit issue (for clothing and footwear)
- Item not needed / change of mind (subject to condition requirements)

Once your return is inspected and approved, a refund or exchange will be processed.

---

### Q1.2: Is the return window always 30 days?

**Answer:**
The return window varies by product category:

| Category | Return Window |
|---|---|
| Electronics & Gadgets | 10 days |
| Clothing & Footwear | 30 days |
| Home & Kitchen | 30 days |
| Books & Media | 15 days |
| Furniture & Large Appliances | 7 days |
| Beauty & Personal Care | 15 days (unopened only) |
| Grocery & Perishables | Not returnable |
| Customized / Personalized Items | Not returnable |

The return window starts from the **date of delivery** as recorded by our logistics partner. Always check the product page for category-specific return policies before purchasing.

---

### Q1.3: What items are non-returnable?

**Answer:**
The following items **cannot be returned or exchanged** once delivered:

- **Perishable goods** — food, beverages, fresh produce
- **Customized or personalized items** — engraved jewelry, custom-printed products, made-to-order furniture
- **Digital products** — software licenses, e-books, digital subscriptions once activated
- **Intimate wear and swimwear** (for hygiene reasons)
- **Hazardous materials** — flammable or pressurized items
- **Opened consumables** — cosmetics, vitamins, supplements (once seal is broken)
- **Mattresses and bedding** (once opened)
- **Items marked "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable"** on the product page

If a non-returnable item arrives damaged or defective, an exception may be made. Contact support with photos within **48 hours of delivery**.

---

### Q1.4: Can I return a sale or discounted item?

**Answer:**
Yes, items purchased on sale or with a discount are **eligible for return** under our standard return policy, unless they are marked **"Final Sale"** or **"Non-Returnable"** at the time of purchase.

**Refund amount for discounted items:**
- You will receive a refund for the **amount actually paid** (i.e., the discounted price), not the original MRP.
- If a coupon was used, the coupon amount is **not refunded** (it may be re-issued as store credit at our discretion — check promotion terms).

## 2. Initiating a Return

---

### Q2.1: How do I initiate a return?

**Answer:**
To start a return:

**Via the website/app:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order containing the item you want to return.
3. Click **"Return Item"** next to the relevant product.
4. Select the reason for the return from the dropdown.
5. Choose your return method (free pickup or drop-off — see Q2.3).
6. Confirm and submit the return request.

You'll receive a confirmation email with your **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number** and next steps.

**Via customer support:**
If you're unable to initiate via the app/website, call **1800-XXX-XXXX** (toll-free) or email **returns@ourstore.com** with your order number and return reason.

---

### Q2.2: Do I need to return the original packaging?

**Answer:**
Yes, whenever possible. Items should be returned in their **original packaging** (box, poly bag, or pouch) with all accessories, manuals, and warranty cards included. This helps us verify the product condition and process your return faster.

If the original packaging is damaged or unavailable:
- Use adequate protective packaging (bubble wrap, sturdy box) to prevent transit damage.
- Do not use only tape to wrap an item without inner padding.
- If the lack of original packaging is our fault (e.g., it arrived badly packaged), note this in your return request.

Returns with missing packaging may be subject to a **restocking fee** or partial refund in some categories (e.g., electronics).

---

### Q2.3: How does the return pickup process work?

**Answer:**
After your return is approved, we'll schedule a **free doorstep pickup** (available in most serviceable areas):

1. You'll receive an SMS/email with the pickup date (typically within 2–3 business days).
2. Ensure the item is packed and ready with the **return label** (downloadable from your account) affixed visibly.
3. Hand the package to our logistics partner's agent at the time of pickup.
4. Collect the **pickup acknowledgment receipt** from the agent.

**If pickup isn't available in your area:**
You'll be asked to **drop off** the item at the nearest courier partner center. We'll provide the drop-off address and pay for return shipping if the return reason is our fault (wrong/damaged item). For change-of-mind returns, a shipping cost deduction may apply.

---

### Q2.4: Can I drop off the return at a physical store?

**Answer:**
If we have any authorized physical store or drop-off locations near you, they'll be listed during the return initiation process. Enter your pin code to check availability.

Not all locations accept returns — only those designated as return-drop points will be listed. Do not ship your return to our corporate office or warehouse directly.

---

### Q2.5: What should I do if the return pickup doesn't happen on the scheduled date?

**Answer:**
If a pickup is missed:

1. Wait until the end of the day — pickups can happen anytime in the pickup window.
2. If not picked up by end of day, go to **My Orders → Return Status → Reschedule Pickup**.
3. Alternatively, call our support line or email **returns@ourstore.com** with your RMA number.
4. We'll reschedule within **1–2 business days** at no extra effort from you.

If your pickup is missed **3 times**, we'll automatically convert your return to a drop-off with a pre-paid return label.

## 3. Return Inspection & Approval

---

### Q3.1: How long does it take to process a return once the item is picked up?

**Answer:**
After the item is picked up or dropped off:

1. **Transit time:** 3–5 business days for the item to reach our returns warehouse.
2. **Quality inspection:** 1–2 business days for a thorough inspection.
3. **Approval/rejection decision:** You'll be notified via email.
4. **Refund initiation:** Within 24 hours of return approval.
5. **Refund credit:** 2–7 business days depending on your payment method.

**Total expected timeline:** 7–14 business days from pickup to refund credit.

You can track the status of your return at any time under **My Account → Orders → Return Status**.

---

### Q3.2: What if my return is rejected after inspection?

**Answer:**
If your return fails our quality inspection, we'll:

1. **Notify you via email** explaining the specific reason for rejection (e.g., item used/worn, missing parts, damage not reported at time of return request).
2. **Return the item to you** at no extra charge.

Common rejection reasons:
- Item shows clear signs of use not disclosed in the return request
- Missing accessories, original packaging, or warranty cards
- Item doesn't match the one we dispatched (wrong item sent back)
- Physical damage that wasn't present at delivery

If you believe the rejection is incorrect, you can **raise a dispute** within 7 days of the rejection notice by replying to the rejection email or contacting support.

---

### Q3.3: What happens if the returned item is damaged during transit back to you?

**Answer:**
We take responsibility for items damaged in transit **when we arrange the return pickup**. If damage occurs:

- Document the damage at the time of handover (take photos before handing to the agent).
- We'll assess the damage during inspection and may still approve the return.
- If we used a courier partner for return logistics, we'll file a claim with them on your behalf.

If you dropped off the return using **your own courier** (without our pre-paid label), you are responsible for safe transit. We recommend using a tracked, insured service and keeping the receipt.

## 4. Exchanges

---

### Q4.1: Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?

**Answer:**
Yes. We offer exchanges for the following reasons:
- **Wrong size or color** received
- **Defective or damaged** product
- **Item doesn't match** the description

**How to request an exchange:**
1. Go to **My Orders → Exchange Item**.
2. Select the item and reason.
3. Choose the replacement (different size, color, or the same item if defective).
4. Submit the request.

The original item will be picked up and the replacement dispatched simultaneously (subject to stock availability). If the replacement item has a price difference, you'll be charged or refunded the difference accordingly.

---

### Q4.2: What if the item I want to exchange to is out of stock?

**Answer:**
If your preferred replacement is out of stock:
- You can **wait for restock** — we'll hold your exchange request for up to **14 days** and notify you when it's back.
- You can opt for a **full refund** instead.
- You can choose an **alternative item** of the same or different value (pay the difference or receive a partial refund).

If we don't expect restocking within a reasonable time, we'll proactively notify you and process a refund.

---

### Q4.3: Can I exchange an item for a completely different product?

**Answer:**
Currently, our direct exchange feature is only for **same product (different variant — size/color)** or **defective item replacement**.

If you'd like to swap for a completely different product:
1. **Return** the original item for a refund.
2. **Place a new order** for the product you want.

If the timelines concern you (waiting for the refund before reordering), you can place the new order and the refund will be credited to your account/original payment method once the return is processed.

## 5. Cancellations

---

### Q5.1: How do I cancel an order?

**Answer:**
**Before dispatch:**
1. Go to **My Account → My Orders**.
2. Select the order you want to cancel.
3. Click **"Cancel Order"** or **"Cancel Items"** (for partial cancellation).
4. Select the reason and confirm.
5. Cancellation is immediate and a refund is initiated within 24 hours.

**After dispatch (but before delivery):**
- You can still request a cancellation, but the item must be **refused at delivery** — do not accept the package.
- Contact support immediately with your order number.
- Refund will be processed after the item is returned to our warehouse.

**After delivery:**
- Cancellation is no longer possible — you'll need to initiate a **return** instead.

---

### Q5.2: What items cannot be cancelled?

**Answer:**
The following cannot be cancelled once the order is placed:

- **Customized/personalized orders** — production begins immediately after order placement
- **Digital goods** — once a download link or activation code is delivered
- **Same-day delivery orders** — if preparation has already started
- **Orders already shipped** — cancellation can only be done by refusing delivery

For COD orders, you can cancel before the item is dispatched without any charge. If you refuse a COD order at delivery, no payment is involved but frequent refusals may limit your COD eligibility in the future.

---

### Q5.3: I accidentally cancelled the wrong order. Can I undo it?

**Answer:**
If you cancelled an order by mistake:

- **Within 15 minutes** of cancellation: Contact support immediately via live chat. If the cancellation is still being processed (order not yet fully cancelled in the system), we may be able to reverse it.
- **After 15 minutes**: Cancellations are typically irreversible. You'll receive a full refund and will need to place a new order. Check if the items are still available.

To avoid accidental cancellations, we recommend double-checking the order number before confirming a cancellation.

---

### Q5.4: How long does a cancellation refund take?

**Answer:**
Cancellation refunds are initiated within **24 hours** of the cancellation being confirmed. Refund timelines by payment method:

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| Credit/Debit Card | 5–7 business days |
| UPI | 2–5 business days |
| Net Banking | 3–7 business days |
| Wallet | 1–3 business days |
| COD | No payment made, no refund needed |
| Store Credit | Immediately on cancellation |

For prepaid orders where the item was already dispatched and refused/returned, add 5–7 additional days for transit time.

## 6. Damaged, Defective & Wrong Items

---

### Q6.1: I received a damaged item. What should I do?

**Answer:**
If your item arrived damaged:

1. **Document the damage immediately** — take clear photos/video of the damaged item AND the outer packaging.
2. **Do not use or install** the damaged item.
3. Go to **My Orders → Report Issue → Damaged Item**.
4. Upload the photos and describe the damage.
5. Submit within **48 hours of delivery** for priority handling.

We'll offer one of the following resolutions:
- **Free replacement** dispatched within 2–3 business days
- **Full refund** to your original payment method
- **Partial refund** for minor damage if you wish to keep the item

For large items (appliances, furniture), we may send a technician to inspect before deciding on replacement vs. repair.

---

### Q6.2: I received the wrong item. What happens?

**Answer:**
We're sorry for the error. If you received an item that doesn't match what you ordered:

1. Go to **My Orders → Report Issue → Wrong Item Received**.
2. Enter your order number and describe what you received vs. what was ordered.
3. Attach photos of the item and its label if possible.

We'll arrange:
- **Free pickup** of the wrong item.
- **Dispatch of the correct item** within 1–2 business days (subject to stock).
- If the correct item is unavailable, a **full refund** will be issued.

You are not responsible for return shipping costs in this case — it is entirely our error.

---

### Q6.3: An item is missing from my order. What should I do?

**Answer:**
If you received a partial delivery (some items missing from a multi-item order):

1. First, check if your items were shipped in **multiple packages** — you may receive separate deliveries.
2. Check your **order details** and tracking — each shipment has a separate tracking number.
3. If after 2 business days beyond the expected delivery date items are still missing, go to **My Orders → Report Issue → Item Not Received**.
4. We'll investigate and either dispatch the missing item or issue a refund within 3–5 business days.

---

### Q6.4: The product I received is different from what was shown on the website. Can I return it?

**Answer:**
Yes. This qualifies as a **listing discrepancy** and is always eligible for a return regardless of product category. When raising the return request:

- Select reason: **"Item doesn't match description/image"**
- Include photos clearly showing the difference
- Our team will verify the discrepancy with the product listing

If the discrepancy is confirmed, you'll receive a **full refund or free replacement**, and the listing will be corrected. We treat this as a priority case.

## 7. Partial Returns & Multi-Item Orders

---

### Q7.1: Can I return just one item from a multi-item order?

**Answer:**
Yes. You can return individual items from a multi-item order. At the return initiation step:

1. Go to **My Orders → [Order Number]**.
2. Click **"Return Item"** next to the specific item you want to return.
3. Complete the return process for that item only.

The refund will be calculated for that item only, plus any applicable prorated shipping deduction. Other items in the order are unaffected.

---

### Q7.2: What if I want to return all items in an order?

**Answer:**
For a full order return, you can either:
- Return each item individually through the return flow (select all items).
- Or, go to **My Orders → [Order] → Return All Items** if that option is available.

A single pickup will be arranged for all items, packaged together. Return the items in separate packaging if they were delivered separately.

## 8. Seller Returns (Marketplace Orders)

---

### Q8.1: Are returns handled differently for marketplace (third-party seller) items?

**Answer:**
For items sold by third-party sellers on our marketplace:

- **Our platform return policy still applies** — sellers listed on our platform must honor our minimum return standards.
- The return process is the same: initiate via **My Orders → Return Item**.
- Refunds are processed by our platform, not directly by the seller.

However, some sellers may have **more generous return policies** than our standard. Check the seller's store page for their specific policy. In disputes, our platform's policy takes precedence and we mediate between you and the seller.
