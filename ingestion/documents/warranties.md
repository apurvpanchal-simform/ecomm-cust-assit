# Warranty Policy

## Overview

Certain products are covered by manufacturer or company warranties.

Warranty coverage varies by product category.

---

## Warranty Coverage

Warranty may cover:

* Manufacturing defects
* Hardware malfunctions
* Component failures under normal use

Warranty does not cover:

* Physical damage
* Liquid damage
* Unauthorized repairs
* Improper use
* Accidental damage

---

## Warranty Periods

| Category    | Coverage  |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Electronics | 12 Months |
| Appliances  | 24 Months |
| Accessories | 6 Months  |

Actual warranty period may vary by product.

---

## Claim Process

### Required Information

* Order ID
* Product Serial Number
* Proof of Purchase
* Description of Issue

### Verification

Support team may request:

* Product images
* Diagnostic logs
* Video evidence

---

## Resolution Options

Approved claims may result in:

* Repair
* Replacement
* Refurbished replacement
* Partial refund

---

## Warranty Transfers

Warranties are generally non-transferable unless explicitly stated.

---

## Extended Warranty

Customers may purchase extended protection plans where available.

Coverage terms are provided separately.

---

## Service Centers

Warranty service may be provided through:

* Authorized service centers
* Manufacturer support network
* Mail-in repair programs
