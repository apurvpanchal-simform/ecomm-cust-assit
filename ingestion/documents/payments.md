# Payment Policy

## Supported Payment Methods

We support:

* Credit Cards
* Debit Cards
* UPI
* Net Banking
* Digital Wallets
* Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)
* Store Credits

---

## Payment Security

All transactions are protected using:

* SSL encryption
* PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways
* Fraud monitoring systems

Sensitive card information is not stored on company servers.

---

## Payment Authorization

When placing an order:

1. Payment request is submitted.
2. Bank verifies transaction.
3. Authorization response is returned.
4. Order confirmation is generated.

---

## Failed Payments

Common reasons:

* Insufficient balance
* Incorrect card details
* Bank restrictions
* Expired card
* Network issues

Recommended actions:

* Retry payment
* Use alternative payment method
* Contact bank

---

## Refund Payments

Refunds are processed to the original payment source whenever possible.

Typical refund timelines:

| Method      | Time      |
| ----------- | --------- |
| UPI         | 2–5 Days  |
| Credit Card | 5–10 Days |
| Debit Card  | 5–10 Days |
| Wallet      | 1–3 Days  |

---

## EMI and Installments

Eligible customers may choose installment options.

Terms vary based on:

* Bank
* Card issuer
* Financing provider

---

## Fraud Prevention

Transactions may be flagged for:

* Unusual purchase behavior
* Multiple failed attempts
* Geographic inconsistencies

Additional verification may be required.

---

## Tax Information

Applicable taxes are calculated during checkout and displayed before payment confirmation.

Invoices include:

* Product cost
* Tax breakdown
* Discounts
* Shipping charges

---

## Currency Support

Supported currencies depend on the customer's region and payment provider.
