# Account Frequently Asked Questions

## Registration

### How do I create an account?

Select Sign Up, provide required information, and verify your email address.

### Is email verification required?

Yes, email verification improves account security.

---

## Login

### I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the "Forgot Password" option to reset your credentials.

### Why is my account locked?

Possible reasons include:

* Multiple failed login attempts
* Security concerns
* Suspicious activity

---

## Profile Management

### How do I update personal information?

Navigate to:

Account → Settings → Profile

and edit the desired fields.

### Can I change my email address?

Yes, verification may be required.

---

## Security

### How do I enable two-factor authentication?

Go to Security Settings and enable 2FA.

Supported methods:

* Authenticator App
* SMS Verification
* Email Verification

---

## Orders and Account

### Can I view previous orders?

Yes, order history is available in your account dashboard.

### Can I download invoices?

Invoices can be downloaded from completed orders.

---

## Account Deletion

### How do I delete my account?

Submit a deletion request through Account Settings or customer support.

### What happens after account deletion?

* Personal data is removed according to policy.
* Access to order history may be limited.
* Recovery may not be possible.

---

## Privacy

### How is my data protected?

Data protection measures include:

* Encryption
* Access controls
* Security monitoring
* Compliance procedures

### Can I request my personal data?

Yes. Customers may request data exports according to applicable privacy regulations.
